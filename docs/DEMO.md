# Recording the demo (3 to 5 minutes)

One story, told twice: **the same scam, met at two moments.** Ramesh meets it early and
stops. Priya meets it late and gets help. This follows section 20 of the [PRD](PRD.md).

## Before you hit record

- Open the live site on a **phone-sized** window (or a real phone) and on a laptop.
- Language: start in **Hindi**. Marathi and English are one tap away in the header.
- Clear any old case: press **+ नया केस शुरू करें** in the top bar (next to the A / A / A buttons).
- Turn the Radar switch **off** (it is off by default).
- Close other tabs. Turn the system volume up, for the read-aloud.

## Click path

| Time | What to do | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Show the SEBI fake-trading-app poster, then the Satark home page | "She never thought it was a scam, because it was working. SEBI draws this scam as a poster. We made it live." |
| 0:25 | Tap **ग्रुप का न्योता**, then **जाँचें** | "Ramesh in Nashik was added to a VIP group. He pastes the invite." |
| 0:40 | The red **रुकिए / STOP** stamp lands. Point at the thread: stage 3 | "STOP, at stage 3. Satark also tells him what comes next: fake profits." |
| 0:55 | Open **क्यों?** on one flag | "Every warning sign has a rule, his own words marked, and the SEBI page behind it." |
| 1:10 | Scroll to **पैसे देने से पहले जाँच**, run it on `rajesh1234@oksbi` | "Not a validated handle. And we say clearly: format is not proof. We send him to SEBI Check." |
| 1:25 | Tap **अपने भरोसे के व्यक्ति को बताएँ** (WhatsApp opens with a ready message) | "Scams live on secrecy. One tap tells his son." |
| 1:35 | Scroll to the box. Add Priya's message: **पैसे निकालने पर टैक्स** | "Now Priya, weeks later, same scam." |

| 2:40 | Show **कोई चेतावनी नहीं मिली** and the line that Satark never says safe | "No red flags found. And look: it never says safe. That is a promise in the code, with a test." |
| 2:50 | Type just **hi**. Show the answer: **पक्का नहीं कह सकते** (cannot verify) | "A message with nothing to judge never gets a good answer." |

| 3:30 | Open **भरोसा रिपोर्ट**, then **ठगी रडार** | "Measured numbers, including the limits. And for regulators: the stage at which people first came to us." |
| 3:50 | Back to the home page | "Pause before you pay." |

## Make sure these are true on the day

- The numbers you say out loud match `/trust` exactly. Read them off the page.
- The radar page shows the **sample data** banner unless real counts have come in. Say so if asked.
- If the optional AI helper is off (no key), do not mention a screenshot upload.
